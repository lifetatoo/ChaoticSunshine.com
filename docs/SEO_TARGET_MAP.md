Already built: unique metadata, canonicals on indexable pages, social metadata on existing content, linked Scripture/topic/resource pages, visible long-form answers, server-rendered content, article/resource/breadcrumb/collection schema, HTML directory and XML sitemap. New schema is descriptive and truthful; no fabricated review score, FAQ rich-result promise, products for free resources, fake publication dates, or invented ratings.

For AI answer/search eligibility, readable crawlable content and normal search foundations matter. Google does not require a special GEO/AEO schema type. Do not treat llms.txt or keyword stuffing as an indexing fix. Official reference: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features

Before public release: domain must resolve; the actual public build must have indexable HTML and no global noindex, its canonical host/redirect must work, and sitemap/robots must be reachable. Keep review builds private/noindex. After approval and publication, verify in Search Console, submit the sitemap and monitor actual queries. Research volume/difficulty and adjust editorial priorities after technical access is reliable.
