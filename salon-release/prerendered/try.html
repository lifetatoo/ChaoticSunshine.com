INTO THE LIFE YOU ALREADY HAVE

Let a little truth
come with you.

Romans 8:12–17 describes belonging to God’s family. What could change in one ordinary moment if you began from that belonging?

One honest prayer.
No impressive introduction.

Try this: before your next prayer, pause. Begin in your own voice. If the word Father is difficult, bring that difficulty honestly, too.

Where could this meet your day?

Why this little experiment? ↓

Connect your practice to the passage. What does Paul say about the Spirit, fear, and being God’s children? Use those verses to check your understanding.

When you come back… ↓

Without rereading first, what do you remember about belonging? Then reopen Romans 8:12–17. What did you notice in the experiment? It is fine if the answer is a question rather than a breakthrough.
